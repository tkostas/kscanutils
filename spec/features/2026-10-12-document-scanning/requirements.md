# Document Scanning — Requirements

- **SCAN-1:** Discover and select a connected scanner.
- **SCAN-2:** Offer 150, 200, 300, and 600 DPI before acquisition.
- **SCAN-3:** Preview each acquired page and rotate it left or right.
- **SCAN-4:** Allow additional pages to be acquired and pages to be removed.
- **SCAN-5:** A one-page scan may be saved as PNG, JPEG, or PDF.
- **SCAN-6:** A scan containing multiple pages may only be saved as PDF.
- **SCAN-7:** Export preserves page order, orientation, and physical dimensions implied by DPI.
- **SCAN-8:** Acquisition and export remain responsive and failures preserve scanned pages.
- **SCAN-9:** Existing outputs require replacement confirmation.
- **SCAN-10:** JPEG export offers standard, high, and maximum quality; PNG is identified as lossless.
